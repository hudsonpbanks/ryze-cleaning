What areas in Utah do you service?

We serve homeowners across Utah County and Salt Lake County. Not sure if we cover your area? Reach out and we'll let you know.

How quickly can I get a quote?

We typically respond to quote requests within a few hours. Fill out our quote form or call/text us directly and we'll get back to you fast — usually the same day.

What services do you offer?

We specialize in exterior window cleaning, interior window cleaning, and commercial window cleaning. We also handle post-construction cleans for new builds.

How often should I have my windows cleaned?

For most Utah homes, we recommend 2–4 times per year. Spring and fall cleanings are especially popular. Homes near dusty roads, with pools, or with lots of foliage may benefit from more frequent cleanings.

Are you insured and professional?

Yes. We are fully insured and our crew is trained, uniformed, and experienced. We take care of your home from the time we arrive to the time we leave.

Do you offer a satisfaction guarantee?

Absolutely. If you're not happy with the results, let us know and we'll come back to make it right at no additional charge. Your satisfaction is our top priority.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

For exterior-only jobs, you don't need to be home — just make sure we have access to all areas. For interior cleaning, we ask that an adult be present during the service.
