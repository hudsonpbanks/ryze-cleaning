## Why Alpine Windows Need More Attention

Alpine is one of the most heavily wooded cities in Utah County — mature cottonwoods, box elders, and maples line nearly every street and border most residential lots. That canopy creates shade and character, but it also produces one of the most sustained pollen and debris loads of any city in the area. From April through September, something is always falling, flowering, or releasing onto glass and frames.

The late May cottonwood drop is particularly significant. Cottonwood cotton carries tannin — a natural compound that stains whatever it settles on. When the cotton packs into window tracks, sills, and frame joints and gets wet, it leaves behind a brownish stain that simple wiping won't remove. Professional cleaning with the right solutions is the only reliable way to lift it without damaging the surrounding surface.

American Fork Canyon is just to the north, and the seasonal winds it channels bring fine canyon particulate into Alpine neighborhoods regularly. Combined with the tree debris load, Alpine homes often need three professional cleanings per year to maintain genuinely clear glass. RYZE is based in nearby Pleasant Grove and serves Alpine on a regular schedule — every job is backed by a 100% satisfaction guarantee.
