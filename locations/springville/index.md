## Springville's Seasonal Window Challenges — and How We Handle Them

Springville earns its Art City nickname — residents here pay attention to how their homes look from the street. That makes dirty windows feel worse than they would anywhere else. The view from the Springville Museum of Art parking lot tells you everything: homes in this city are kept up. Your windows should be too.

The local conditions don't help. Hobble Creek Canyon funnels wind from the east through the spring and fall, carrying fine particulate from the foothills directly onto east-facing glass. Utah's dry summer heat means there's rarely enough rain to rinse mineral deposits away — they build up on sprinkler-hit surfaces faster than most homeowners realize, and harden into the surface if left too long.

We clean Springville homes with the same process we use across Utah County: full scrub, squeegee, hand-dry on every pane — inside and out. No windows skipped. How does Utah's dry heat affect window clarity in Springville?

Low humidity means mineral deposits from sprinkler systems and rain have no natural rinse cycle. They accumulate on glass through the summer and harden under heat. By fall, those deposits are significantly harder to remove than they would have been in spring. Cleaning in both spring and fall breaks that cycle.

Do you serve homes near Hobble Creek Canyon?

Yes. We serve Springville's east-side neighborhoods including homes along the Hobble Creek Canyon corridor. Canyon proximity typically means more wind-driven dust on east-facing glass — something we account for when quoting and cleaning those properties.

What cleaning schedule works for a two-story Springville home?

Most two-story homes benefit from exterior cleaning twice a year — late spring and early fall. If you have a lot of south-facing glass, summer glare from dirty windows becomes noticeable enough to push some homeowners to add a mid-summer exterior touch-up. We can recommend a specific schedule after seeing the home.

Do south-facing windows show streaks more than others?

Yes. Low-angle sun in spring and fall catches any residue left on glass and makes it visible from inside the house. We hand-dry every pane after squeegeeing — most services skip this step, which is exactly where streaks come from. It adds time but it's the only way to get genuinely clean results on south-facing glass.

Do I need to be home for an exterior-only cleaning?

No. For exterior-only jobs you just need to ensure we have access to all sides of the home. We send a confirmation the day before, complete the work, and notify you when we're done. For interior cleaning, an adult needs to be present during the service.
