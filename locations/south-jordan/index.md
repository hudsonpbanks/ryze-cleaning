## South Jordan Is Growing — and New Windows Need the Right First Clean

South Jordan has been one of Utah's fastest-growing cities for over a decade. Daybreak alone added thousands of homes, and construction across the city continues. New builds look great on move-in day, but they almost always come with a layer of construction dust, concrete overspray, and adhesive residue from protective window film — none of which comes off with a standard cleaning. We handle post-construction cleans regularly in the area and know what's needed to get glass truly clear from day one.

Beyond new construction, South Jordan's established neighborhoods sit against the Oquirrh Mountains to the west. Prevailing westerly winds carry fine mineral dust from the range into the valley, and west-facing windows accumulate it faster than other exposures. The Jordan River corridor on the east side adds humidity that contributes to mineral deposits from condensation over time.
